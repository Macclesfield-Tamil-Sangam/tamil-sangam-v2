Who we are

Macclesfield Tamil Sangam (“the Sangam”, “we”) is a volunteer-run, non-profit community group in Macclesfield, Cheshire. We are responsible for the personal information collected through this website.

What we collect

  • Membership: your name, email address, phone number (optional) and password (stored securely, we cannot see it).
  • Event bookings: your name, email, phone (optional), the tickets you booked and whether you have paid or checked in.
  • Contact form: your name, email, phone (optional) and your message.

We do not receive or store your card details. Card payments are processed by Stripe.

Why we use it

  • To manage your membership and let you log in (with your consent).
  • To manage event bookings, send confirmations and check people in on the day (to provide the booking you asked for).
  • To reply to your messages.
  • To tell members about Sangam events. You can ask us to stop at any time.

Who else handles it

We use trusted services to run the website: Supabase (database and logins, stored in the EU), Netlify (website hosting and contact form), Stripe (card payments) and an email service for confirmations. They only process your information on our behalf. We never sell your information or share it for marketing.

How long we keep it

Membership details are kept while you are a member and deleted within 12 months of you leaving. Booking records are kept for up to 2 years for our accounts. Contact messages are deleted within 12 months.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or object to how we use it. Contact us through the contact page. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

Cookies

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The site stores your language choice and, if you log in, your login session in your browser.
